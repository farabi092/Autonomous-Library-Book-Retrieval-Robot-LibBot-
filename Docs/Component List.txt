LibBot - COMPONENTS LIST


MICROCONTROLLER:
- 1x Arduino Uno

MOTORS & DRIVERS:
- 2x DC Motors (3-6V, ~100 RPM)
- 1x L298N Motor Driver Module
- 2x Wheels 
- 1x Caster wheel 

SENSORS:
- 1x 5 Array IR Sensors (line detection)
- 1x HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor
- 1x MFRC522 RFID Reader Module
- 5x RFID Cards/Tags

ACTUATORS:
- 4x SG90 Servo Motors (for arm)

POWER:
- 1x External 5V/2A Power Supply
- 1x 9V Battery (optional, for Arduino)
- 1x Buck Converter
- Jumper wires, breadboard, USB cable

CHASSIS:
- Robot chassis 
- Servo mounting brackets
- Arm structure (3D printed)
- Gripper mechanism

MISC:
- Buzzer 
- Switch
- Hot glue gun for assembly
- Cable ties/zip ties
